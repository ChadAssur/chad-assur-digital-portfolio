# TaskLink Logo Guidelines

## Logo Concept and Rationale

### Refined Logo Concept
After evaluating all options, the "Community Handshake" logo was selected as it powerfully embodies TaskLink's entire brand ethos, mission, and target audience needs. It represents the promise the platform makes to its users.

### Design Rationale
The logo features two hands clasped in a handshake, with the forearms subtly forming the negative space of a link or a heart shape at the junction. This directly represents the "link" in TaskLink and the caring, community-focused nature of the service.

### Design Elements
- **Symbol:** Community Handshake icon with integrated link/heart negative space
- **Wordmark:** "TaskLink" set in clean, bold, friendly sans-serif typeface (Montserrat Bold)
- **Aesthetic:** Modern, approachable, conveying professionalism and reliability
- **Scalability:** Effective in large formats and when reduced to tiny favicon

## Logo Lockup and Variations

### Primary Logo (Full Color Horizontal)
- **Usage:** Default and most used version
- **Application:** Headers, main marketing collateral, digital platforms
- **Construction:** Icon to the left of the wordmark

### Stacked Logo
- **Usage:** Ideal for vertical spaces
- **Application:** Mobile app headers, social media profiles, square materials
- **Construction:** Icon centered above the wordmark

### Icon (Brand Mark)
- **Usage:** Extremely limited space applications
- **Application:** Favicon, app icon, social media profile picture
- **Construction:** Handshake/link icon alone

## Usage Standards

### Safe Zones (Exclusion Zone)
To protect logo integrity and visibility, a minimum clear space must surround the logo on all sides, defined by the height of the letter "T" in the wordmark (referred to as x).

- **Minimum Clear Space:** 1x on all sides of the logo lock-up
- **Protection:** No other graphic elements, text, or borders should encroach this area

### Minimum Sizes
To ensure legibility and avoid distortion:

- **Print Applications:** 1.0 inch (25.4 mm) wide for primary horizontal logo
- **Digital Applications:** 60 pixels wide for primary horizontal logo
- **Icon-Only:** 24 x 24 pixels for digital use (e.g., favicon)

## Color Applications

### Primary Full-Color Version
- **Icon:** Gradients or solid fills using primary brand blue (#2E86AB)
- **Wordmark:** Solid primary blue (#2E86AB)
- **Background:** White or very light grey for optimal contrast

### Reverse (Light) Version
- **Usage:** Dark or photographic backgrounds
- **Color:** Entire logo reversed to solid white
- **Background:** Dark backgrounds with sufficient contrast

### Single-Color Version
- **Usage:** Color-unavailable applications
- **Options:** Solid Black or Solid White
- **Applications:** Faxes, embossing, debossing, single-color print

## Unacceptable Usage

### Prohibited Practices
- Do not place full-color logo on busy backgrounds reducing readability
- Do not recolor the logo outside approved palettes
- Do not use low-contrast color combinations
- Do not distort, rotate, or modify logo proportions
- Do not add effects like shadows, glows, or bevels
- Do not separate icon from wordmark unless using icon-only version

### Quality Standards
- Always use high-resolution versions
- Maintain proper color values as specified
- Ensure adequate contrast with backgrounds
- Preserve logo proportions and spacing

## Implementation Guidelines

### Digital Applications
- Websites: Use PNG format with transparent background
- Social Media: Follow platform-specific size requirements
- Presentations: Maintain minimum size requirements
- Documents: Use high-resolution embedded images

### Print Applications
- Marketing Materials: Use vector formats (AI, EPS)
- Business Cards: Ensure minimum size compliance
- Signage: Scale appropriately for viewing distance
- Merchandise: Follow specific substrate requirements

## Brand Consistency

The TaskLink logo is the visual cornerstone of our brand identity. Consistent and proper application across all touchpoints is essential for building brand recognition, trust, and professional reputation in the marketplace.
