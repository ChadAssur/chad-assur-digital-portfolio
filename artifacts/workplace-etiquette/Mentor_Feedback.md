## STAR Reflection
**Situation:** Receiving and implementing mentor feedback was essential for professional growth, skill development, and meeting workplace expectations during my internship.

**Task:** I needed to actively seek constructive feedback, objectively assess areas for improvement, and systematically implement changes to enhance my performance and professional development.

**Action:** I regularly requested feedback from my mentor and carefully considered all suggestions provided. I analyzed feedback patterns to identify recurring themes and priority areas. For each feedback point, I developed specific action plans with measurable outcomes. I implemented changes systematically and tracked my progress. I also sought clarification when feedback wasn't specific enough to ensure I understood expectations clearly.

**Result:** The feedback implementation led to significant professional growth and skill development. My punctuality and reliability became consistently praised attributes, and my technical skills, particularly in mockup design, developed to a level specifically highlighted as exceptional. The process transformed generic workplace expectations into specific, demonstrated competencies that will benefit my future career.
