## STAR Reflection
**Situation:** Transitioning from academic environment to professional workplace required significant adaptation and development of new skills, behaviors, and mindsets appropriate for corporate settings.

**Task:** I needed to rapidly develop professional competencies including workplace etiquette, quality standards, time management, and corporate communication while maintaining academic performance and delivering quality project work.

**Action:** I actively observed workplace norms and sought guidance on professional expectations. I implemented strict personal discipline around attendance, punctuality, and work quality. I sought regular feedback and made continuous adjustments to my approach. I studied professional communication standards and practiced business-appropriate interactions. I took ownership of my skill development by identifying gaps and seeking learning opportunities.

**Result:** I successfully transformed from a student mindset to a professional orientation, demonstrated by consistent positive mentor feedback and quality work delivery. I developed strong workplace habits including perfect attendance, reliable deadline meeting, and professional communication. This transformation has prepared me for successful transition to full-time professional roles and established a foundation for ongoing career development.
