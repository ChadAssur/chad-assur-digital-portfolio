## STAR Reflection
**Situation:** Building a professional online presence through LinkedIn was essential for establishing industry connections, showcasing my qualifications, and accessing career opportunities in the IT sector.

**Task:** I needed to create a comprehensive LinkedIn profile that effectively represents my skills and experience, then actively build a professional network and engage with the IT community.

**Action:** I optimized my LinkedIn profile with a professional headline, detailed experience descriptions, and relevant skills. I connected with CPUT alumni, industry professionals, and project collaborators. I joined IT-focused groups and participated in discussions to build visibility. I regularly shared updates about my TaskLink project progress and internship learnings to demonstrate continuous professional development.

**Result:** I established a growing professional network that provides valuable industry insights and potential opportunities. My LinkedIn profile now serves as an effective digital resume that has attracted connection requests from recruiters and industry professionals. The networking efforts have expanded my understanding of the IT job market and created pathways for future career development.
