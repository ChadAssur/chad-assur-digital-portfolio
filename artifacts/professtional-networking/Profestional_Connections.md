## STAR Reflection
**Situation:** Building and maintaining professional relationships was essential for career development, industry insights, and accessing opportunities in the competitive IT job market.

**Task:** I needed to establish meaningful connections with IT professionals, academic contacts, and industry influencers while developing relationship management skills for long-term professional networking.

**Action:** I actively sought connections through LinkedIn, industry events, and academic networks. I prepared for networking interactions by researching individuals' backgrounds and preparing relevant questions. I maintained regular communication through thoughtful updates and value-sharing. I attended virtual industry events and participated in discussions to increase visibility. I also sought mentorship relationships for guided professional development.

**Result:** I built a diverse professional network that provides ongoing learning opportunities and career support. These connections have offered valuable industry insights, career advice, and potential job leads. The networking skills developed will continue to benefit my career growth long-term and have already opened doors to opportunities I wouldn't have accessed otherwise.
